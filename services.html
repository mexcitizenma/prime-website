Interior Painting

Living rooms, bedrooms, hallways, kitchens, bathrooms, basements and ceilings. We patch nail holes and cracks, caulk the trim, sand where it matters and cut clean lines, then protect your floors and furniture for the whole job.

  • Walls, ceilings, trim, doors and closets
  • Drywall patching, caulking and priming included
  • Accent walls and color consultations
  • Popcorn ceiling repaint and basement walls
  • Furniture covered, hardware removed and refit
Get a free estimate

Exterior Painting

Curb appeal that survives a New England winter. Surfaces are washed, scraped and primed before a single coat goes on, because prep is what decides whether a finish lasts two years or ten.

  • Siding, clapboard, shingles and stucco
  • Trim, soffits, fascia and shutters
  • Porches, railings, decks and steps
  • Wash, scrape, sand and spot-prime prep
  • Exterior-grade paints and stains
Get a free estimate

Cabinet Painting & Refinishing

A brand-new kitchen look for a fraction of the cost of replacing the boxes. Doors and drawer fronts are removed, degreased, sanded, primed and sprayed for a smooth factory-style finish.

  • Kitchen and bathroom cabinets and vanities
  • Doors removed, labeled and sprayed off-site or on-site
  • Degrease, sand and bonding primer on every surface
  • Durable enamel finish built for daily use
  • New hinges and hardware installed on request
Get a free estimate

Fence Painting & Staining

Wood fences dry out fast in the Valley sun. We clean, prep and coat them so the color stays even and the wood stops soaking up water.

  • Privacy, picket and post-and-rail fences
  • Solid stain, semi-transparent stain or paint
  • Pressure washing and drying time included
  • Gate hardware checked and adjusted
  • Deck and pergola staining as well
Get a free estimate

Garage Epoxy Floor Coatings

A sealed garage floor sweeps clean in minutes and stops soaking up oil and road salt. We grind or etch the concrete first so the coating actually bonds instead of peeling in sheets.

  • Garages, basements, workshops and utility rooms
  • Surface prep, degreasing and crack filling
  • Solid color or decorative flake finish
  • Resistant to oil, salt, chemicals and hot tires
  • Clear topcoat option for extra durability
Get a free estimate

Pressure Washing

Years of dirt, pollen and green growth come off in an afternoon. We match the pressure to the surface so nothing gets gouged or stripped.

  • Driveways, walkways, patios and pool decks
  • Decks, porches, stairs and fences
  • Garage floors and concrete pads
  • Pre-paint washing for exterior projects
  • Soft-wash approach on delicate surfaces
Get a free estimate

Siding Cleaning

House looking dull and streaked with black or green? A proper soft wash lifts mildew and algae off vinyl and painted siding without forcing water behind the panels.

  • Vinyl, aluminum and painted wood siding
  • Mildew, algae and pollen removal
  • Gutter face and trim cleaning
  • Low-pressure soft wash technique
  • Great before listing a home for sale
Get a free estimate

Laminate Flooring Installation

Fast, clean flooring upgrades for rooms that need a fresh start. We handle the underlayment, cuts and transitions so the finished floor lays flat and quiet.

  • Laminate and click-lock vinyl plank
  • Underlayment and moisture barrier
  • Baseboard, quarter round and transition strips
  • Doorway and stair-edge trimming
  • Old flooring removal and haul-away
