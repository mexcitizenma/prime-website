Questions

Common questions

What areas do you serve?

Westfield, Agawam, Springfield, West Springfield, Southampton, Holyoke, the surrounding Western Massachusetts towns and nearby communities in neighboring states. If your town is not on the list, text and ask — we often travel a little farther for the right job.

Are estimates really free?

Yes, and there is no obligation. Send a few photos through Messenger for a ballpark number, or book a walkthrough for an exact written quote.

How soon can you start?

Most estimates are scheduled within a couple of days. Smaller jobs — a single room, a fence, a TV mount — can often be booked the same week.

Do you take on small jobs?

Definitely. One room, one cabinet door, a garage floor or a short handyman punch list are all welcome.

Who supplies the paint and materials?

We do, and the brand and finish are listed on your estimate. If you have a color or product you already love, we are happy to use it.
